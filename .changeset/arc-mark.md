Add `Chart.Arc`, a mark for pie and donut charts. It draws one slice of a circle per row, clockwise from the top. The name of each slice gives its share of the total, the data table adds a share column, and the legend lists the categories in the order of the slices. `inner` makes a donut, and `labels` shows the value in each slice when it fits.
